How AI Metadata Works & How GhostMeta Cleans It

Everything you need to know about AI provenance, C2PA, and privacy protection.

🛡️ What is C2PA / Content Credentials?

C2PA is an open technical standard used by OpenAI (DALL·E 3, ChatGPT, Sora), Adobe (Firefly), Google, Microsoft, and camera manufacturers. It embeds a cryptographically signed manifest directly inside JPEG APP11/APP2 markers or MP4 UUID boxes, binding the image or video to an AI provenance certificate. GhostMeta safely neutralizes these manifests without altering picture pixels.

🔍 What is Google SynthID?

Google DeepMind embeds SynthID into Gemini and Imagen outputs in two ways: first via standardized IPTC/XMP tags (such as digitalSourceType: trainedAlgorithmicMedia), and second through subtle frequency-domain watermarks. GhostMeta strips the metadata headers in Lossless mode, and its Deep Canvas Scrub applies micro-frequency dither to neutralize spectral watermark correlation.

🎨 Midjourney & Stable Diffusion Prompts

Stable Diffusion, ComfyUI, and Automatic1111 embed complete prompts, negative prompts, seeds, and full node workflows inside PNG tEXt/iTXt chunks and JPEG EXIF comments. Midjourney embeds job IDs and prompts. GhostMeta surgically removes these text chunks, keeping your creative prompts confidential.

🎬 Video Metadata (Runway, Sora, Pika)

AI video generators embed model tags, generation prompts, and encoder signatures inside MP4 and MOV moov > udta and uuid container atoms. GhostMeta transforms these atoms into compliant free filler atoms, ensuring instant zero-recompression cleaning without desynchronizing video chunk offsets.

🔒 100% Client-Side Privacy

Your images and videos are never uploaded to any remote server or cloud. Every byte is parsed, analyzed, cleaned, and re-packaged right inside your browser’s JavaScript engine using high-performance ArrayBuffer and Canvas APIs.

⚡ Lossless vs Deep Scrub

Lossless Clean strips metadata containers directly at the binary byte level, guaranteeing 100% bit-for-bit identical visual quality and instantaneous speed. Deep Scrub draws onto a fresh Canvas to destroy all non-pixel artifacts and steganography.
